## 1. THE ANSWER (what Axoworks actually is)

**40-word version:** A two-principal Washington building-design practice that runs like a corporate team at the cost of one — half of it designs and permits local work; the other half is the hidden BIM/AI department behind other firms' drawings; and its own website is the proof.

**Full version:** Axoworks LLC (Kingston, WA — principals Kee and Tina Song) is two firms in one body:

| Half | What it does | Who it's for | Canon name |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Design firm** | Feasibility & zoning, custom homes/ADUs/multifamily, permit documentation (IBC, IRC, IECC-WA, NFPA, ADA — sealed via licensed partners) | Homeowners, developers, WA State | The Power of One |
| **Shadow fulfillment & retooling** | White-label BIM/AI production behind other firms' letterhead; private LLM deployment; verification stacks; hybrid AutoCAD→Revit | Architects & AEC firms | Ghost in the Machine |

The unifying asset is **The Concierge** — the live AI agent that *is* the company website, exposed as an MCP/A2A endpoint so AI agents call the firm directly. The marketing thesis in one line, their own: **"The technology is the receipt. The buildings are the work."** (proof-of-work, 2026-09-14)

**Identity = refusals** (each one is positioning): not a licensed architecture firm ("Independence as Strategy"); not a technology company; not a SaaS vendor (owned harness, no subscriptions); not generic AI consultants ("tech bros who've never read a zoning code"); not national (WA-only permitting).

## 4. THE CENTRAL PARADOX — and the resolution line

**Stealth vs. spotlight.** The brochures promise "Absolute Stealth… they don't need to know we exist" while the firm publicly bylines a canon, self-crowns "first AEC firm in history" (⚠ self-reported superlative), and publishes its own security wounds.

**Resolution (use verbatim-ready):** *Invisible to your clients. Famous to your industry.* The ghost wins work precisely because it publishes receipts — the tech receipt is what makes the invisibility credible. Corroborated by the corpus ("You're a subcontractor, not a firm." "Yes, and that is the point.") but never yet stated in one place. **State it in one place.** (See Fix List.)

Secondary reconciliations, same pattern: local-first is a *product they sell*, not a property of their cloud storefront; "not a service provider" (q05) vs. the seven-line service map (q07) — resolve toward service provider with a philosophy, not the reverse.

## 6. VOICE DNA (the editor's checklist)

**Three registers, one spine:** Hype-Man (2025) → Manifesto (Dec 2025–Jan 2026) → Adversarial Auditor (Jun–Sep 2026). Ship only in the third register now, with the Manifesto's defiance retained.

**Always:**
- Number-first ledes ($0.74; $1; 215+ probes).
- Coinage with receipt: "(n.) … Coined by AxoWorks."
- Named adversarial casts (gladiators, suspects, dogs, Honey Badger).
- Self-adversarial disclosure ("Confession:" blocks) — trust manufactured by exposing your own gaps.
- "X, not Y" aphorism pairs: "a floor, not a moat," "Installed, not ranked," "a screen, never a source of truth."
- Jargon always pays a translation toll ("grandma could get" → "The 40-Word Answer").
- Disclosure line: "drafted with three AI models, cross-examined against each other, edited by humans who sign it."

**Platform-scoped (added 2026-09 from the LinkedIn export):** on **LinkedIn and X**, functional emoji and a 5–8 hashtag cluster are house style, and the canonical link goes in the **first comment** framed as a dare or a reward. In a **canonical article**, emoji and hashtags remain at zero. See `AXOWORKS-LINKEDIN-VOICE.md` and `AXOWORKS-CONTENT-STRATEGY.md` §3B.

**Also from the export — his own best one-liners, reuse verbatim:** *"Replacing headcount with agents that survive real building code."* · *"Human judgment."* · *"Facts. Not a vibe."* · *"You read this far. You earned it."* · *"As far as we know, the first in our industry … tell us if we're wrong."* Fresh credential for bios: **Cooper Union alumnus, 30 years Arch+Build** (currently absent from `llms.txt` — see Fix List).

**Never:** unverified superlatives; hidden failures ("It's on our To-do list" is the standard); stock boilerplate; a sentence a machine can't check; flattened doctrine for a "safe" audience.

## 8. FIX LIST (credibility repairs, cheapest first — all verified in corpus)

1. **Give "Explore the Axosphere" a destination** before any agent-facing play ships (Plays 1–3). No such surface exists in the corpus — only "Initialize The Concierge at axoworks.com" (2026-01-01). Build it or swap the CTA.
2. **Lead llms.txt + Concierge service map with the 50/50 split.** The most distinctive fact about the firm is disclosed in exactly one article. Two lines in llms.txt fixes the biggest legibility gap for machines and humans alike.
3. **Reconcile "5-layer" vs L0–L5 (six layers).** llms.txt + news say "5-layer verification stack"; the canonical article defines six layers, L0–L5. Pick one count everywhere — it's the flagship framework.
4. **Deploy or retire "Power of One."** Currently hero-only, zero article support, Concierge improvises an exegesis. One canonical essay makes it load-bearing (it's the natural homeowner/design-half flag).
5. **State the stealth/spotlight reconciliation once, explicitly:** "Invisible to your clients. Famous to your industry." One paragraph on the Concierge/About-equivalent ends the drift between q04 ("invisible → deliberately visible") and q05 ("the client never knows we exist").
6. **Correct the surviving self-contradiction** in from-portfolio-to-agent ("independent audit… 8.625/10") against its own published correction (post_p07, 2026-07-02: self-initiated DeepSeek review). The firm's own doctrine — "fails out loud" — demands it.
7. **Stop using "first AEC firm in history."** Their own retrospective concedes it's "difficult to verify." The verifiable version is stronger: "one of the first documented attempts to make an AI agent the entire website of a professional services firm."
8. **Date hygiene:** JSON-LD datePublished is refresh-dated (several articles describe events months after their nominal date). Set `dateModified` honestly and let the news timeline carry provenance — machines currently reconcile these and find drift.
