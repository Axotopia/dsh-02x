## 2. Positioning Architecture

**Elevator answer.** Axoworks LLC is a two-principal building-design practice in Kingston, Washington that operates like a much larger firm by making AI do the department work: half the practice designs and permits homes, ADUs, and small multifamily for Washington clients (with licensed partners sealing where required), and the other half works invisibly as the BIM/AI production department behind other firms' drawings. They proved the model on themselves by deleting their own website on January 1, 2026 and replacing it with an AI agent — The Concierge — which is simultaneously storefront, portfolio, and evidence that a tiny team can ship corporate-grade infrastructure. (Synthesis of: "AxoWorks (n.)" definition and "50% design firm · 50% shadow fulfillment" table, proof-of-work.md, 2026-09-14; llms.txt: "AI-augmented research, design, and pre-construction consultancy… Our homepage is a live multi-model AI terminal ('The Concierge')"; INFERENCE on the self-demonstration framing.)

- **Service layer (what clients buy):** feasibility/site analysis with pro formas; design + permit documentation; hybrid AutoCAD-to-Revit delivery ("You control the design, we wrestle the Tech"); private/on-prem LLM deployment for AEC firms; pre-construction risk audits; engagement "project-based, hourly consulting, or embedded workflow partnerships" (llms.txt Services; q07 service map). No published fees: "I don't publish a fee schedule — scopes and fees are set by the principals" (q07).
- **Technology layer (what they built):** The Concierge (Brain-Hands-Voice loop, Eager RAG, two-persona tone system) exposed as MCP + A2A endpoints; revit-tools MCP server with "deterministic geometry audits"; the L0–L5 verification stack; Referee-in-the-Pit debate team; $1 Property Report preset; the Coercing-AI-Compliance render pipeline. Their ledger maps each tool to a building problem: "Every tool traces to a building problem. None would exist without the practice drawing buildings first." (proof-of-work.md §7).
- **Philosophy layer (why they win):** deterministic rails on stochastic systems; handler skill over model quality (Three Dog); own the loop, don't rent the dashboard ("stop renting neutered sub-intelligence with a pretty face," fight-club.md); publish proof, not brochures ("The receipt is on the GitHub, not on the parapet"); the human keeps four irreducible points — "Premise, Scope, Verification, Seal… The seal stays human" (proof-of-work.md §§6, 9). The productive paradox: they sell *corporate* output while despising corporate overhead — "the headcount fallacy" (seventy-four-cent-website.md).
- **Identity layer (what they refuse to be):** **not a licensed architecture firm** — "We're a design and tech shop, not a licensed architecture or engineering firm" (q01); refusal as strategy: "Independence as Strategy… lets us bypass legacy trade bureaucracy… while a State-licensed Architect of Record handles the seal" (q08). **Not a technology company** — "You're a technology company." "No. The technology is the receipt; the buildings are the work." (proof-of-work.md §9). **Not a SaaS/subscription vendor** — owned harness, "No subscription, no lock-in" (the-machine-wears-the-harness.md). **Not a generic AI consultancy** — "generic tech bros who've never read a zoning code" (q08). **Not national** — "Permitting and on-site compliance work happens in Washington State" (q01, q07).

## 4. Tensions

1. **Stealth vs. spotlight.** Sell side: "Absolute Stealth (White-Label Execution)… We remain invisible, they don't need to know we exist" (post_brochure02.md, 2026-01-05); "The client never knows we exist" (q05). Public side: bylined Intelligence Logs, self-crowned "first AEC firm in history to ship a public-facing AX interface" (news_timeline.md, 2026-01-01), and "You're a subcontractor, not a firm." "Yes, and that is the point." (proof-of-work.md §9). The reconciliation — invisible to *their* clients, famous to the *industry* — is real but never stated in one place. INFERENCE.
2. **The 50/50 vs. every other self-description.** llms.txt defines them as an "AI-augmented research, design, and pre-construction consultancy"; q07's service map never mentions that half the work is hidden fulfillment; only proof-of-work.md (2026-09-14) discloses "50% design firm · 50% shadow fulfillment & BIM/AI retooling." A stranger reading llms.txt or asking the Concierge (q01) gets the design-firm face; the fulfillment-engine half appears in exactly one late article.
3. **"Not a service provider" vs. the service map.** "We're not a service provider. We're the invisible technical wing" (q05) directly conflicts with llms.txt Services and q07's seven-line service list. The Concierge contradicts the brochure-era pitch depending on which corpus node answers.
4. **Privacy fortress vs. cloud Concierge.** "The Privacy Fortress. Local LLMs. On-premise deployment. Client CAD/BIM data never leaves their hardware" (q05; axo_kb\local-llm-privacy.md, 2025-06-15) vs. their own public agent running on Netlify Edge calling DeepSeek/OpenRouter/Tavily/Moonshot APIs (llms.txt CSP allow-list), with Eager RAG "tokens burned regardless" (q03). Their unstated defense: local-first is a *product they sell*, not a property of their storefront. INFERENCE.
5. **"5-layer" vs. L0–L5.** Marketing consistently says "5-layer verification stack" (llms.txt; news_timeline.md, 2026-08-10) while the canonical article defines six layers, L0–L5 (verification-stack.md §8). Small, but it is their flagship framework.
6. **Self-corrections that contradict surviving canon text.** from-portfolio-to-agent.md (2026-01-15) still prints "An independent audit scored this system 8.625 out of 10," while the correction post rules: "The evaluation was a self-initiated automated code review conducted by DeepSeek… That framing is inaccurate and needs correction" (post_p07.md, 2026-07-02). Likewise the "first in history" claim is flagged by their own article as "a self-reported claim that is difficult to verify" (from-portfolio-to-agent.md, 2026-01-15). Corpus datelines are also unstable: magic-pill.md fm 2025-05-08 vs. body "February 2026"; seventy-four-cent-website.md fm 2025-12-15 vs. narrated events of Jan–Feb 2026 and body dateline "August 2026."

**Bottom line:** the canon is coherent at depth — *tiny team, deterministic rails, receipts over brochures, human keeps the seal* — but the surface vocabulary was written for four different listeners at once, and the firm's own admission of being 50% hidden fulfillment engine is the one fact its storefront still doesn't lead with.
