## Phase 1 — Concierge / Agentic Launch (Dec 2025 – Apr 2026)

Ideological groundwork first: "AI Fight Club: Why Tiny Teams Beat Corporate AI" (axo_kb/fight-club.md, 2025-12-12) — "stop renting neutered sub-intelligence… Your arena. Your game." — the founding statement of the multi-model, anti-subscription doctrine. The Concierge was built "End of 2025" (axo_kb/seventy-four-cent-website.md, 2025-12-15), persona tuning took "2.5 months" (ibid.), and Axoworks 2.0 launched December 31, 2025: "We have initiated the Agentic Web for 2026… The first deployed public-facing Agentic Interface in the industry… We are defining AX (Agent Experience)" (axo_kb/site_nodes/news_timeline.md, 2025-12-31). January 1, 2026: "We didn't 'update' our website. We deleted it… We replaced the entire digital existence of a professional firm with one thing: The Concierge. A production-grade Autonomous Intake Node"; "Tiny is Mighty" debuts as hashtag (news_timeline, 2026-01-01). Same-day article three-dog-theory (2026-01-01): "Are you running a shelter, or are you part of an Elite Unit?" The Concierge ran Claude Opus 4.5 until a Feb 1 hot-swap to DeepSeek R1 after Edge migration broke translation ("We don't patch with poetry. We patch with logic." — news_timeline, 2026-02-01). Self-run "hostile" audits scored 6.5 → 8.0 → 8.625 (Jan 28–Feb 19), later self-corrected: "An LLM reviewing code at its creator's request is not an independent third-party audit" (axo_kb/site_nodes/post_p07.md, 2026-07-02). Show HN (March 19, 2026, 43 comments) acted as a red team: PII exposure scrubbed, "bulldog" persona toned down (axo_kb/from-portfolio-to-agent.md, §7; axo_kb/site_nodes/post_p07.md). Build-log concierge-logs (2026-03-01) added crawler-swarm defense against "OpenClaw" agents. Through this window the old service lines were reprinted as white-label brochures ("Your Ghost In The Machine… SEAL Team for Impossible Deadlines… They don't need to know we exist" — axo_kb/site_nodes/post_brochure03.md, 2026-01-05) and six of the 2025 articles became podcasts (axo_raw/corpus/posts_summary.jsonl, p01–p06, Jan 12–Feb 19, 2026). Tension: the firm simultaneously sells invisibility (ghost brochures) and maximum visibility (agent-first site).

## Conflicts & Open Questions

- **Dates are unreliable as first-publication markers.** from-portfolio-to-agent is JSON-LD-dated 2026-01-15 but its byline says "July 2026" and it describes a May 2026 sitemap inspection and the March 19, 2026 HN thread; three-ways-ai-lies is dated 2025-05-10 yet announced July 20, 2026; seventy-four-cent-website is dated 2025-12-15 while narrating the January 1, 2026 launch and a 30-day post-mortem. Treat date_published as canonical-sitemap metadata, not provenance.
- **HN thread details conflict:** 48 comments / "#2 on the HN front page" (seventy-four-cent-website) vs 43 comments / 20 points / March 19, 2026 (from-portfolio-to-agent, §7).
- **"First AEC firm in history to ship a public-facing AX interface"** (news_timeline, 2026-01-01) is self-reported; their own retrospective concedes "a self-reported claim that is difficult to verify" (from-portfolio-to-agent, §1.2).
- **"Power of One"** appears only in home.html meta tags ("Delivering the workload of a corporate team at the cost of One"); the Concierge delivers an elaborate exegesis (axo_kb/mcp/q02_canon.json) but no article supports it — likely a legacy site tagline the KB absorbed.
- **Concierge identity drift:** q05_future still leads with white-label stealth ("The client never knows we exist"), contradicting its own q04 claim of moving "From invisible → to deliberately visible," and both sit atop the Phase-3 self-promotional "proof of work" positioning. The corpus reconciles this only partially, as a 50/50 practice split (proof-of-work, 2026-09-14).
- **Undated nodes:** all 25 project files carry no dates (only Cloudinary upload IDs); the corpus gives no Axoworks LLC founding date and no primary evidence for the "launched as a white-label BIM-AI strike team" origin the Concierge asserts (axo_kb/mcp/q04_evolution.json) — earliest attested service content is April 2025.
