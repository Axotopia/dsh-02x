## 2. POST ANATOMY — the repeatable LinkedIn shape

Eighteen posts collapse into a consistent machine. Learn it and the voice follows.

```
1. HOOK — one short line. A declaration, a dare, or a price.
     "Game's over. Pay-to-play? Only for laggards."
     "Are you running a firm, or a shelter?"
     "$1. That's what a property report costs now."
     "The AI Vulcan mind meld is live. 🖖"

2. THE MECHANISM — what was actually built, in one or two sentences.
     "We built a fight club to catch our own models lying:"

3. NAMED CAST or A LABELED LIST — emoji-fenced, one line each.
     🛡️ The Advocate — argues FOR, no mercy.
     ⚔️ The Prosecution — argues AGAINST, won't concede.
     🦡 The Honey Badger — takes no side, just brings receipts.
     ⚖️ The Referee — scores it.

4. RECEIPTS — cost, count, or a section literally headed "Receipts:" or "Cost?"
     "Cost? 13 model calls."
     "Receipts: Full research report ≈ $1. Zoning gut-check ≈ cents. Local weights? ≈ free."
     "That answer cost $0.0024 — 28.9K tokens, 47 seconds. (Receipt in the first comment below.)"

5. AUDIENCE SEGMENTATION — emoji-labeled, one line per persona (posts 3 and 16).
     🏛️ Architects — code checked in minutes, not days. Citations attached.
     🏢 Developers — pro-forma & IRR stress-tested before the bank meeting.
     🏠 Homeowners — title, parcel & setback traps flagged before escrow.
     💻 IT / Ops — headless pipelines that triage and self-heal on your own metal.

6. CTA — link in FIRST COMMENT, stated as a dare or an earned reward, never a plea.
     "Link to the full technical breakdown and GitHub repo in the comments. 👇"
     "Repo in 1st comment. You read this far. You earned it."
     "Try to break it in the first comment. 👇"
     "No catch." / "No strings." / "It's yours!"

7. A CLOSING APHORISM OR PUNCHLINE — often a P.S.
     "The machine wears the harness now. You just talk — it clicks."
     "TINY is MIGHTY. Believe it!"
     "P.S. Google's agent helps you reschedule a haircut. Ours helps you permit a skyscraper in Mandarin. One is convenience. The other is conviction."

8. HASHTAGS — 5–8, functional, one cluster at the foot.
```

**Measured across the 18 posts:** median length **~107 words** (range 42–231). Hashtags 0–8 (median 5 when present). Emoji 0–8 (median ~2). **Length discipline is tight** — the modal LinkedIn post is under 120 words, well inside the 100–250 budget.

## 6. WHAT THIS CHANGES IN PRACTICE

1. **Hashtags and functional emoji are ON for LinkedIn and feed platforms.** The style profile's blanket prohibition must be read as *canon-scoped*. On LinkedIn: 5–8 hashtags at the foot, emoji as bullet/step/audience markers, `👇` before a comment CTA. In a canonical article: still none. Never decorative emoji anywhere.
2. **The comment-CTA is the house move.** "Link in first comment" — never "link in bio," never a bare URL in the body as the ask.
3. **"Receipts:" and "Cost?" are named sections.** He literally writes the heading. Use them.
4. **Audience-segmentation bullets work well for him.** Four emoji-labeled personas, one line each — a repeatable format for posts that speak to multiple buyers.
5. **Substack (`axotopia.substack.com`) and Medium are live syndication surfaces.** Reconcile every platform plan against them.
6. **His own best one-liners should be reused verbatim.** *"Replacing headcount with agents that survive real building code."* *"Human judgment."* *"Facts. Not a vibe."* *"You read this far. You earned it."* These are canon-grade and they are his.
