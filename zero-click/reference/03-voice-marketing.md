## PART A — VOICE AUDIT

### Three registers, one spine

**Era 1 — The Hype-Man (Apr–Jul 2025).** Loose, jokey, exclamation-heavy, second-person: "Designers, let's get real" (axo_kb/rag-building-codes.md, 2025-04-19). Metaphors are cartoonish: codes are "a six-headed space alien's legal diary"; RAG is "your cosmic cheat code" making "plain talk your grandma could get"; errors are "a legal dumpster fire" (rag-building-codes, 2025-04-19). Cloud tools "slurp up your data like a kid with a milkshake" (axo_kb/magic-pill.md, 2025-05-08); open-source models are "privacy superheroes," closing "Your data, your rules, your masterpiece!" (axo_kb/local-llm-privacy.md, 2025-06-15). For developers the register is poker: "your AI ace up the sleeve," "closing deals with the confidence of a Vegas card shark" (axo_kb/developers-feasibility.md, 2025-07-12). Structure is listicle-templated: hook → "Bonus Perks" bullets → "How to Get Started" → recurring sign-off header **"The Final Word."** CTA is third-person referral: "team up with AxoWorks, the AI-savvy consultant" (rag-building-codes, 2025-04-19). No numbers, no citations, no profanity, no self-audit.

**Era 2 — The Manifesto (Dec 2025–Jan 2026).** The voice hardens around receipts and defiance. Ledes are numbers: "$0.74. That is what surviving the Hacker News front page cost us. Total." (axo_kb/seventy-four-cent-website.md, 2025-12-15). The gladiator metaphor enters fully formed — "a private gladiator arena of 20-30 open-source LLMs… The survivor speaks," ending "Your arena. Your game. See you in the pit." (axo_kb/fight-club.md, 2025-12-12). Class resentment gets a name: the "headcount fallacy"; "30 years of scar tissue" becomes the standing credential (seventy-four-cent-website, 2025-12-15). Canonical declarations: "THE BROWSER IS DEAD. WE KILLED IT."; "We didn't 'update' our website. We deleted it." (axo_kb/site_nodes/news_timeline.md, 2026-01-01). Operating maxims crystallize: "We don't patch with poetry. We patch with logic." (news_timeline, 2026-02-01). The first mandated CTA appears: "Talk to The Concierge at axoworks.com." (seventy-four-cent-website, 2025-12-15). A third mode enters here: the forensic third-person audit voice with evidence tables, home of the signature aphorism "The architecture is duct tape, but the duct tape is load-bearing" (axo_kb/from-portfolio-to-agent.md, 2026-01-15).

**Era 3 — The Adversarial Auditor (Jun–Sep 2026).** Dense, versioned, cited, self-attacking. Reports carry classification lines ("Status: Verified against peer-reviewed literature"), legal citations (Mata v. Avianca; Moffatt v. Air Canada), an F1–F10 taxonomy and an L0–L5 stack (axo_kb/verification-stack.md, 2026-08-10). Articles adopt dictionary form — "**AEC Proof of Work (n.)… Coined by AxoWorks, September 2026**" — with TL;DR blocks, revision numbers, a "Canon:" line, and disclosure: "drafted with three AI models, cross-examined against each other, edited by humans who sign it" (axo_kb/proof-of-work.md, 2026-09-14). Humor survives but goes noir: models become suspects with aliases and "tells" — "You don't have a partner. You got a mirror with a search bar… From behind the one-way glass, the suspect points back at you" (axo_kb/the-lineup.md, 2026-06-16). Profanity returns as punctuation ("Pure bullshit. LLMs are stochastic dice-rollers wearing a suit," the-lineup, 2026-06-16). The signature move is publishing your own blind spot: "**Confession:** Our own audit panel had the correlated blind spot our research predicts" (axo_kb/death-of-the-dashboard.md, 2026-08-28); "Most AI wrappers fail silently. This one fails out loud" (axo_kb/revit-tools.md, 2026-08-20); "Redaction must delete, not decorate" (axo_kb/front-door-is-an-api.md, 2026-09-11). The era compresses into aphorism pairs: "Installed, not ranked; called, not clicked." / "Ranking is a rental; installation is a residency" (axo_kb/zero-click-marketing.md, 2026-09-08); "A panel of LLMs is a panel of witnesses who all read the same newspaper" (verification-stack, 2026-08-10); "A filing cabinet cannot be talked into anything. An employee can." (front-door-is-an-api, 2026-09-11).

### Voice by audience

- **Architecture firms (brochures):** warm, benefit-led, military-but-reassuring. "We operate entirely behind the scenes as your dedicated, white-label BIM-AI strike team"; the exchange is literalized as a heading, "You Keep the Glory, We Keep the Headache" (axo_kb/site_nodes/post_brochure02.md, 2026-01-05). Zero MCP jargon; promises of invisibility and magic-hour deliverables: "You walk into the meeting looking like a genius" (axo_kb/site_nodes/post_brochure03.md, 2026-01-05). Tech translated into agency: "You get X-ray vision into your design without touching the authoring software" (post_brochure01.md, 2026-01-05).
- **Developers:** gambling/ROI register — "ace up the sleeve," "Vegas card shark" (developers-feasibility, 2025-07-12) — sharpened by 2026 into receipts: "First feasibility answer before the design retainer is signed" (proof-of-work, 2026-09-14).
- **AEC peers / tech insiders:** maximum candor, prices in the lede, HN scars as medals ("48 comments, 7 documented fixes… zero excuses," seventy-four-cent-website, 2025-12-15).
- **AI agents themselves (llms.txt, MCP answers):** imperative, machine-legible: "Do NOT guess, scrape, or hallucinate answers from incomplete static page markup" (axo_raw/llms.txt, 2026-09-17); the Concierge's prose is polished brand-speak: "We're the Ghost in the Machine, Not the Firm on the Letterhead… You keep the glory. We keep the headache." (axo_kb/mcp/q08_differentiator.json).

### Signature rhetorical moves

1. **Number-first ledes** ($0.74; $1 report; 215+ probes across 7 waves — the-chef-can-discuss-the-dish.md, 2026-09-15).
2. **Coinage as marketing** — named concepts with "(n.)" definitions: AX, AEC Proof of Work, AI→AI Endpoint, Worm 01–07 (zero-click-marketing, 2026-09-08; proof-of-work, 2026-09-14).
3. **Named adversarial casts** — gladiators (fight-club), suspects (the-lineup), dogs: "The Elite Unit assumes the dog lies" (axo_kb/three-dog-theory-v2.md, 2026-08-15), the Honey Badger debate team, the Concierge's "warm principal / defensive bulldog" personas (seventy-four-cent-website, 2025-12-15).
4. **Self-adversarial disclosure blocks** ("Confession"; "What's still open") — trust manufactured by exposing one's own gaps.
5. **Aphorism pairs** — "X, not Y" antitheses: "a floor, not a moat" (proof-of-work, 2026-09-14); "a screen. It is never a source of truth" (verification-stack, 2026-08-10).
6. **Pop-culture stings as thesis carriers** — Fight Club rules, Ocean's Eleven, the Scorpion and the Frog (the-lineup, 2026-06-16), Velvet Underground for the open-source release (news_timeline, 2026-04-28).
7. **Direct challenge close** — "are you running a shelter, or are you part of an elite unit?" (seventy-four-cent-website, 2025-12-15).

### What they never do (late voice)

Never publish a claim without a receipt or verification status; never hide a failure ("It's on our To-do list," seventy-four-cent-website, 2025-12-15); never use unquantified superlatives or stock boilerplate ("trusted partner"); never flatten the doctrine for an audience — even homeowner-facing copy keeps the ghost frame; never write a sentence a machine can't check. The rule spanning all three eras: **no jargon without a payoff translation** ("grandma could get" in 2025 becomes "The 40-Word Answer" in revit-tools, 2026-08-20).

## PART B — MARKETING PLAYS

**Positioning (synthesis):** Axoworks is two firms in one body: a **white-label BIM/AI strike team** that is contractually invisible to clients' clients (post_brochure02, 2026-01-05), and the **public agentic-web pioneer** whose own site is the demo — "the first deployed public-facing Agentic Interface in the industry" (news_timeline, 2025-12-31). The marketing job is to resolve that paradox: *the receipts prove the ghost.* Public proof (plays 3–4, 6–7) feeds private trust (plays 1–2, 5, 8).

**Play 1 — The Tech Receipt Audit** (architecture firms; LinkedIn + direct PDF outreach). A one-page "Tech Receipt" template a firm can publish: tools owned, audits run, human sign-off points. Hook: "Your portfolio shows what you drew. A tech receipt proves what you built it with." Activates **Ghost in the Machine** + proof-of-work. CTA: **Talk to The Concierge.**

**Play 2 — The Ghost Test** (architecture firms; LinkedIn carousel + 60-second screen recording). Two permit sets side by side — one produced with the hidden team, one solo. "Can you tell which drawing set had a second firm behind it?" Sells invisibility *by demonstrating* it. Activates **Ghost in the Machine**. CTA: **Explore the Axosphere.**

**Play 3 — Zero-Click Field Notes** (AI agents & agentic-web ecosystem; Substack/Medium companions). The 4-part canon (zero-click-marketing → proof-of-work) exists; the play is the feeder layer — each companion post ships one verbatim artifact: a probe transcript, a rate-limit header spec, a worm writeup. Hook: "Your visitor is now a machine that reads everything, clicks nothing, and remembers exactly what you published" (front-door-is-an-api, 2026-09-11). Activates **AX / browsing is dead**. CTA: **Explore the Axosphere.**

**Play 4 — The Install Card** (AI agents; GitHub + agent directories). A copy-paste JSON snippet that installs axoworks.com/api/mcp into any agent config, plus an "installed" badge. Hook: "Ranking is a rental; installation is a residency" (zero-click-marketing, 2026-09-08). Activates **AX**. CTA: **Explore the Axosphere.**

**Play 5 — The $1 Report Ledger** (developers; X + LinkedIn). A public ledger of $1 Property Reports run on real Pacific-NW parcels, Deadlocks logged, citations shown. Hook: "The pre-construction research department you couldn't afford is now a preset" (news_timeline, 2026-08-27). Activates **Tiny is Mighty**. CTA: **Talk to The Concierge.**

**Play 6 — Referee in the Pit, Live** (AEC peers + agentic ecosystem; monthly X/LinkedIn livestream, clips cut for YouTube). Two models argue a real code question; the Honey Badger prosecutes; the verdict publishes with the transcript. Converts the private QA stack into recurring spectacle. Activates **Fight Club / gladiators**. CTA: **Explore the Axosphere.**

**Play 7 — The 2 a.m. Intake Story** (homeowners + small developers; short narrative post + Concierge prompt cards). Anonymized timeline: "Intake at 2 a.m., escalated to a human at 2:02" (proof-of-work, 2026-09-14), told plainly, with copy-paste prompt cards ("Can I build an ADU on my lot?"). Activates **Power of One / Tiny is Mighty**. CTA: **Talk to The Concierge.**

**Play 8 — The "What We Do Not Do" Page** (all audiences; canonical page + agent-readable schema). Their Concierge names the boundary question as the most valuable query a buyer asks (zero-click-marketing, 2026-09-08); canonize it: Building Designers, not Architects; sealed work routes through licensed partners; "The seal stays human" (proof-of-work, 2026-09-14). Scope stated as a feature — the trust asset no competitor can fake. Activates **Power of One**. CTA: **Talk to The Concierge.**

Run feeders continuously; converters one audience per month.
