### 2.3 USE ONLY WITH AN EXPLICIT HEDGE

| Claim | Required hedge |

| Any unverifiable superlative | Model the hedge on Kee's own: *"As far as we know… We cannot prove that, and we would rather be corrected than quoted."* |

### 2.4 PROVENANCE GRADES (use these words)

- **[Confirmed]** — fetched this session from a primary source; quote matches.
- **[Confirmed-as-printed]** — verified as printed on an official surface, but the claim is the firm's own self-report.
- **⚠ UNVERIFIED** — source blocked, paywalled, login-walled, or unmatched. Always name the reason.
- **⚠ BLOCKED** — e.g. LinkedIn returns HTTP 999 (zero bytes). X/Twitter not publicly retrievable. Do not imply you have read them.

## 4. DISCLOSURE DUTY

The technical canon (Tier 2) is **AI-drafted and says so**: *drafted with three AI models, cross-examined against each other, edited by humans who sign it.* Keep that line. It is not a liability; it is the receipt for the prose, and it is consistent with the firm's entire doctrine.

Never present Tier 2 prose as Kee's personal writing, and never present Tier 1 roughness as the firm's editorial standard. They are two instruments, deliberately tuned.
